# Free Time – brand

One page. If a line of copy does not fit here, rewrite the copy.

## Name

**Free Time.** Positive, says what you get. Always two words, capitalised.

## Lines

| Role | Line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | *The app that rewards you for not using it.* | Hero on README, store listing, website, login screen |
| Explainer | *Points for leaving your phone alone.* | Right under the headline, app description, email footer |
| Short | *Put it down. Score.* | Widgets, notifications, social posts, stickers |
| Category | *an anti-screen-time game* | Search terms, store keywords, FAQ. Never as a headline. |

The headline is a paradox and explains itself. The explainer tells how. The
short line is the game. The category line exists for people searching
"screen time app"; it describes us, it is not our voice.

## Voice

- **Warm, not preachy.** We never shame. "The phone has become a guest that
  never goes home", not "you are addicted".
- **A game between friends, not a court.** Cheating is possible and that is
  fine. We reward, we do not police.
- **Concrete.** Minutes, points, multipliers. Not "mindfulness" or "wellness".
- **Plain words.** Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No "unlock your
  potential".
- **Honest about limits.** iPhone cannot measure everything. We say so.

Avoid: *anti*, *detox*, *addiction*, *dopamine*, *mindful*, *journey*, *unlock*.

## Store copy

**Google Play short description (80 chars):**
The app that rewards you for not using it. Points for leaving your phone alone.

**App Store subtitle (30 chars):**
Points for ignoring your phone

**Keywords:** screen time, digital wellbeing, phone addiction, focus, offline,
anti screen time, presence, friends, leaderboard

## Look

- **Palette "moss & cream":** moss `#1F5F4A`, amber `#E8A33D`, cream
  `#F4F1EA`, ink `#17201C`. Dark mode: night `#0F1412`, mint `#7FD1A8`.
- **Type:** Manrope. Heavy weights for numbers and headlines, tight letter
  spacing.
- **Mark:** a ring, open at the end, with an amber dot in the middle. The
  ring is the day filling up. The dot is you.
- **Shapes:** large radii (16–24), no shadows, no gradients in the UI (the
  ring's sweep is the one exception).
